# My Study Habits and Learning Preferences

## 1. My Academic Background

I am a seventh-semester BS Computer Science student at the University of Management and Technology (UMT).

My academic work involves different areas of computer science, including programming, database management, artificial intelligence, human-computer interaction, and parallel and distributed computing.

I work on assignments, presentations, practical labs, and semester projects. These activities help me explore how theoretical concepts are implemented in real software systems.

## 2. How I Prefer to Study

I prefer studying a topic in a structured and gradual way. I usually need to understand the basic concept before moving towards more advanced details.

My preferred learning sequence is:

1. First, understand what the topic means.
2. Learn why the concept is needed.
3. Understand how it works.
4. Explore a practical example.
5. Try implementing it myself.
6. Identify mistakes and understand how to correct them.
7. Review the concept in my own words.

This approach helps me build understanding instead of memorizing information temporarily.

## 3. Importance of Practical Examples

Practical examples play an important role in my learning.

For example, when studying database relationships, I prefer understanding them through a website scenario with actual entities and tables.

When learning SQL Server, I prefer creating databases, tables, users, and permissions in SSMS and then testing whether the permissions work.

When learning Python, I prefer running scripts and observing their output instead of only reading code.

I find it easier to remember a concept when I have personally performed the steps involved.

## 4. How I Approach Technical Assignments

I prefer starting an assignment by understanding its requirements and expected output.

After that, I divide the assignment into smaller sections. For practical assignments, I like to complete one stage, verify that it works, and then continue to the next stage.

For example, when building a software project, I prefer the following order:

- Understand the project requirements.
- Prepare the development environment.
- Organize project folders and files.
- Implement the main functionality.
- Test the implementation.
- Fix errors.
- Prepare documentation and screenshots.

I also prefer keeping screenshots of important steps when an assignment requires a practical report.

## 5. How I Use AI Tools for Learning

I use AI tools as learning assistants to understand unfamiliar concepts, troubleshoot technical problems, explore project ideas, and receive step-by-step guidance.

However, I prefer explanations that help me understand the work rather than simply copying an answer.

I find it useful when an AI assistant explains why a particular command, library, model, or technical approach is being used.

I also believe that AI-generated information should be checked, especially when it involves technical details or academic research.

## 6. My Approach to Presentations

When preparing an academic presentation, I prefer content that is simple, accurate, and directly connected to the project.

I do not want slides filled with complicated terminology that I cannot explain confidently.

I prefer understanding the complete project first and then preparing short explanations for each slide.

Practical examples and simple scenarios help me explain technical ideas more naturally.

## 7. My Challenges While Learning

When a topic contains many unfamiliar technical terms or several steps are explained together, I may need additional clarification.

I sometimes need to revisit a concept from its basics before understanding its advanced application.

I also prefer not to move to the next implementation stage until the current stage is working correctly.

I consider these part of my learning process and try to improve through practice and repetition.

## 8. My Current Learning Goals

My current learning goals include:

- Improving my Python programming skills.
- Understanding artificial intelligence and retrieval-augmented generation.
- Exploring local and cloud-based language models.
- Improving my UI/UX design and prototyping abilities.
- Learning workflow automation with n8n.
- Strengthening database design and SQL skills.
- Becoming more confident in building and explaining complete software projects.

## 9. What Helps Me Learn Best

The learning methods that work best for me are:

- Clear explanations in simple language.
- Step-by-step demonstrations.
- Real-world examples.
- Hands-on implementation.
- Understanding the reason behind technical decisions.
- Testing and troubleshooting.
- Revising concepts in my own words.

My overall goal is to become a more independent learner who can understand a problem, choose an appropriate solution, implement it, and explain how it works.
